# ICAT v3.2 Page Map

Existing v3.1 pages are preserved. Added organizer and admin-contact pages:

- `admin-contact.html` — Admin requests / complaints / feedback to organizer
- `organizer.html` — Organizer overview
- `organizer-teams.html` — Add/manage teams
- `organizer-players.html` — Add players
- `organizer-access.html` — Grant/revoke captain admin access
- `organizer-rosters.html` — Organizer-only roster Excel upload
- `organizer-schedule.html` — League schedule Excel upload
- `organizer-inbox.html` — Admin requests, complaints and feedback

Admin-only live streaming remains `live-studio.html` and targets the ICAT YouTube channel `https://www.youtube.com/@ICAT-FT20`.

- `admin-schedule.html` — Captain/Admin team entire season schedule

- `availability.html` — Member-only self availability posting
- `schedule.html` — Member-only team season schedule
